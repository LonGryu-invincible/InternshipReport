### Overall Evaluation

**1. Working Environment**  
I was very satisfied with the learning environment provided by the **First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ)** program. Beyond AWS labs, I had opportunities to attend technical events and work on a team project with interconnected components. CPAWS allowed me not only to learn about AWS but also to see how an AI idea becomes a forecasting problem, an evaluation process, and a user-facing system.

**2. Support from Mentor / Team Admin**  
Our mentor was **Mr. Triệu Quốc Hào**. He answered technical questions, provided architectural feedback, helped guide the project, and supported the team when challenges arose. I valued this approach because it helped us address problems while leaving room for independent research and discussion. In my area of Modeling & Evaluation, I had opportunities to explain and discuss my technical proposals.

**3. Relevance of Work to Academic Major**  
As a student interested in artificial intelligence, I found **Modeling & Evaluation** particularly relevant to my academic interests. I contributed to the specification of forecasting outputs, including proposing **log returns rather than absolute price levels**. My subsequent work went beyond experimenting with a single model: it included comparing DLinear, K3, and S1a with the baseline, reviewing metrics, applying time-based evaluation rules, and interpreting results. This helped me view machine learning as a more complete research and verification process. My additional work on Cost Estimation & Optimization complemented this experience by connecting AI research with the operational and financial requirements of a cloud-based system.

**4. Opportunities for Learning and Skill Development**  
For me, some of the most valuable experiences in CPAWS were contributing to evaluation methodology, reviewing technical decisions, and personally carrying out the **official test2020**. Through **Rolling Accuracy**, I learned how published forecasts must be matched with observed data, why provenance matters, and why metrics should only be published when enough valid observations are available. The official test taught me the importance of using a frozen configuration, auditing artifacts, and reporting both findings and statistical limitations.

In the final reporting phase, I also prepared the AWS cost estimate and bilingual cost report for CPAWS, using AWS Pricing Calculator, Cost Explorer, and CloudWatch. Estimating a low-traffic operating scenario at USD 5.26/month taught me to distinguish measured usage from workload assumptions, identify cost drivers, and assess optimization options without claiming unverified savings. This broadened my understanding of the practical requirements of operating an AI system on AWS.

AWS labs and events such as AWS Cloud & AI Day and Cloud Mastery also broadened my knowledge of cloud systems and emerging AI applications.

**5. Teamwork and Collaboration**  
I am relatively outgoing and comfortable participating in discussions about Modeling & Evaluation. I spent most of my time completing tasks assigned by the team leader and collaborating with teammates responsible for data, APIs, infrastructure, and reporting. Reviewing pull requests and technical contracts helped me recognize how seemingly small changes in data handling or evaluation formulas can affect other components.

**6. Policies and Benefits for Interns**  
I appreciated the learning opportunities, technical events, and guidance available through the program. For my own experience, the most important factors were the quality of support, opportunities for hands-on practice, and clear project requirements. I do not have sufficient firsthand information to assess financial benefits or policies outside my direct experience.

### Additional Questions

**What did you appreciate most about the internship?**  
What I appreciated most was contributing to several stages of a practical AI project. I was involved in problem formulation, model research and comparisons, evaluation design, result verification, cost estimation, and preparing evidence for reporting. Personally executing the official test2020 made the responsibility of evaluation especially clear: results should be accurate, auditable, and interpreted carefully. Preparing the cost report and handing it over to the team also helped me understand the importance of operational feasibility. Mentor support and teamwork were valuable parts of the experience.

**What could the program improve for future interns?**  
I was **very satisfied** with FCAJ. One possible improvement would be to consolidate guidance on balancing AWS labs, project development, and reporting earlier in the program. A centralized checklist of key milestones and required evidence could also make progress tracking easier. These are suggestions for the experience of future participants, not a claim that improving the program was part of my assigned role.

**Would you recommend FCAJ to your friends? Why?**  
Yes. I would recommend FCAJ to students interested in cloud computing and AI, particularly those who want to learn through practical projects. The program helped me strengthen independent learning, team communication, and my understanding of how research connects to product development. I would also tell prospective participants to prepare for self-directed study and careful time management.

### Suggestions and Expectations

**Do you have any suggestions for improving the internship experience?**  
I suggest organizing submission requirements, milestones, and evidence criteria in a single, easy-to-use resource. Short question-and-answer sessions about common technical challenges in AWS projects, including usage-based cost estimation and interpreting AWS Pricing Calculator results, could also be helpful. I see these as constructive suggestions. My primary contributions to CPAWS were in **Modeling & Evaluation**, complemented by **Cost Estimation & Optimization** in the final reporting phase.

**Would you like to participate in similar programs in the future?**  
If suitable opportunities arise, I would like to join further research activities, workshops, and projects in Cloud & AI. In the longer term, I hope to pursue research in **artificial intelligence and Robotic AI**, while gaining the practical experience needed to build useful AI systems.

**Any additional comments?**  
FCAJ helped me understand that trustworthy machine learning outcomes depend not only on the models themselves but also on problem formulation, data quality, evaluation methodology, responsible use of cloud resources, and teamwork. I would like to thank the organizers, our mentor **Mr. Triệu Quốc Hào**, and my CPAWS teammates for their support and collaboration throughout the program.
