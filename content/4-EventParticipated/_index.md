During my FCAJ internship, I attended three events: two professional Cloud and AI sessions and one volunteering activity. Each report describes the topics I explored, my personal experience, and the lessons I learned. Supporting photos and video footage are included in the event reports.

### [Event 1 — AWS Cloud and AI Day: Ho Chi Minh City Watch Party](4.1-event1/)

- **Date:** September 29, 2026 · **Location:** 26th Floor, Bitexco Financial Tower, Ho Chi Minh City
- **Role:** Attendee
- **Topics:** Cloud and AI in business, Agentic AI, data foundations, and application modernization.
- **My takeaway:** Evaluating an AI system involves data quality, reliability, and operations, not only model performance.

### [Event 2 — Smart Plushies for Children](4.2-event2/)

- **Date:** October 26, 2026 · **Location:** 36th Floor, Ho Chi Minh City
- **Role:** Volunteer, two-person team
- **Activities:** Completing zippered stuffed bears using materials supplied by the organizers and testing sound responses to gentle stroking and shaking.
- **My takeaway:** Functional testing should reflect real user interactions, and product quality requires careful checks.

### [Event 3 — Cloud Mastery 1: Amazon Bedrock AgentCore](4.3-event3/)

- **Date:** November 14, 2026 · **Location:** 26th Floor, Bitexco Financial Tower, Ho Chi Minh City
- **Role:** Attendee
- **Topics:** AgentCore Runtime, Gateway, Memory, Identity, and Observability.
- **My takeaway:** Building AI agents involves system design, tool management, and monitoring beyond model selection.
